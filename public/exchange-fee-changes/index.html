Crypto Exchange Futures Fee Change Log

Every entry records what changed in a public futures fee schedule, or that we re-checked it and found it unchanged, with the date and the official page. It is the maintenance log behind the fee calculator and the comparison pages, published so that a fee number can always be traced to when it was last checked.

How to read this log

Added means an exchange entered the dataset for the first time. Changed means the exchange published a new schedule or notice on that date. Re-verified means we compared the modeled ladder with the official page on that date and recorded the result. Every number in the calculator carries the date of the last such check.
